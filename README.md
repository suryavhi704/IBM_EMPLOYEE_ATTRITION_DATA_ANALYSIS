# 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is a major challenge for large organizations, impacting productivity, morale, and long-term business growth.
This project analyzes IBM Employee Attrition Data to identify key drivers of employee turnover and convert insights into actionable business strategies using Power BI.

# 🎯 Objectives

Identify key factors contributing to employee attrition

Analyze attrition patterns across demographics, roles, and experience

Provide data-driven insights for HR and leadership decision-making

Recommend strategies to improve employee retention and satisfaction

# 🛠 Tools & Technologies

Power BI – Dashboard creation & visualization

Excel / CSV – Data source

DAX – Measures & KPIs

# 📊 Key Metrics Analyzed

Total Employees: 1470

Employees Left (Attrition): 237

Average Job Satisfaction: 2.47

Average Salary Hike: 15.21%

Attrition by Age Group, Department, Salary Hike, Job Satisfaction, Overtime, Marital Status

# 🔎 Key Insights

High attrition occurs during early career (0–5 years) and around 10 years of experience

Research & Development (R&D) department shows the highest attrition

Employees doing overtime with low job satisfaction (≈2.5) leave more frequently

Male employees contribute more to attrition than female employees

Single employees have a significantly higher attrition rate

Lower salary hikes directly correlate with increased attrition

# 💼 Business Recommendations

Introduce career progression & salary review programs at 5-year and 10-year milestones

Improve work-life balance by managing overtime in R&D

Implement employee engagement initiatives for early-career professionals

Design retention strategies for single employees, such as flexibility benefits

Use attrition prediction insights to guide HR policy and compensation planning
